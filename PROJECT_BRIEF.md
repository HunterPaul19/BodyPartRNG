# Project Brief

## Game

**Body Part RNG**

## High-Level Concept

Body Part RNG is a Roblox collecting and progression game inspired by the feel of games like Sol's RNG.

Instead of rolling auras, players roll for body parts. Each body part has its own rarity and can also carry gameplay modifiers that improve the player's ability to keep progressing.

The core appeal is the excitement of repeated rolls, chasing rare outcomes, and slowly building a stronger setup that increases the chances of landing even better parts.

## Core Loop

1. The player rolls for body parts.
2. The player equips or uses better body parts they obtain.
3. Owned or equipped body parts also generate money passively over time.
4. Better body parts provide useful modifiers such as increased luck or faster roll speed.
5. Passive income and improved stats help the player progress more efficiently and chase rarer parts.
6. The player uses earned money to buy better rules and unlock stronger cases/openings that can grant better body parts.
7. The loop repeats as the player pushes toward increasingly rare collections.

## Progression

- Progression is primarily driven by luck scaling, passive income growth, and access to stronger roll sources.
- Early progression should feel fast and rewarding.
- Mid and late progression should focus on chasing increasingly rare body parts.
- Rarer body parts should meaningfully improve the player's rolling potential, collection value, or both.
- Passive earnings should create a steady sense of forward momentum even between major hits.
- Money should be an important secondary progression currency that helps players buy better rules over time.
- Unlocking better cases/openings should be a major progression milestone.

## Body Parts

- Body parts are the main collectible and progression item.
- Every body part should have a clear rarity/value within the progression ladder.
- Body parts can include gameplay modifiers.
- Body parts can also contribute to passive money generation, reinforcing their long-term progression value.
- Example modifiers:
  - `+Luck %`
  - `+Roll Speed %`
  - Other progression-oriented bonuses as the design expands

## Economy

- Money is earned passively through collected or equipped body parts.
- Passive income should scale with progression so stronger collections feel more valuable over time.
- Money gives players another way to advance by letting them buy better rules in addition to relying on lucky rolls.
- The economy should support the main RNG loop rather than replace it.

## Design Direction

- The game should feel satisfying, readable, and highly replayable.
- Rolling should be quick and exciting.
- Progression should strongly support the fantasy of gradually becoming luckier and more efficient.
- Systems should be designed so new body parts, modifiers, and cases can be added cleanly over time.

## Initial Product Intent

- Build a strong core rolling/progression loop first.
- Keep the first implementation focused on body part rolling, modifiers, and progression.
- Treat cases/openings as a key extension of the main loop rather than a separate system.
- Favor scalable data-driven systems so content can expand without heavy rewrites.
